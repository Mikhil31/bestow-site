Assessment & Risk Analysis

We combine experience with a structured qualitative methodology to assess security risks for residential apartments, corporate buildings, industrial complexes and critical infrastructure. Our team identifies threats, assets and vulnerabilities as precursors to determining security risks.

Our experts then formulate mitigation plans in alignment with client risk tolerance and business operations — covering perimeter control, entry points, high-value assets, staff movement patterns, and emergency scenarios such as fire, medical incidents or unauthorized access.

Typical assessment outputs

  • Site vulnerability mapping and guard post recommendations
  • Access control and visitor management procedures
  • CCTV and patrol route optimization
  • Incident response and escalation protocols
  • Training gaps and deployment staffing levels

Training & Education

We develop customized security training programs for staff in security awareness, threat recognition, fraud awareness, personal protection and client-specific security protocols.

Training varies from traditional classroom and one-on-one courses to multimedia, computer-based and simulation exercises. Security personnel are recruited on an all-India basis against adequate educational qualifications, with strict background checks before induction and deployment.

Classroom Training

Duties, conduct, legal awareness, reporting standards and professional behaviour on site.

Fire Fighting

Practical extinguisher use, evacuation leadership and coordination with fire safety systems.

Rescue Operations

Emergency extraction procedures and scene safety until professional responders arrive.

First Aid

Stabilising casualties, CPR basics and handover to medical teams.

Women Safety

Safe escort protocols, sensitivity training and gender-focused incident reporting.

Our full-fledged training infrastructure includes extensive physical security training, fire fighting, first aid, and fire safety awareness programmes for client employees where required.
