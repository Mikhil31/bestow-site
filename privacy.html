Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit our website or get in touch.

Last updated: May 2026. This is a general privacy notice for visitors to the Bestow Security Services website.

1. Introduction

Bestow Security Services ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or contact us.

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and message content when you use our contact form or reach out by phone or email.

We may also collect basic technical data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited through standard server logs or analytics tools.

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide security services, improve our website, and communicate with clients and prospects. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality and understand how visitors use the site. You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers who assist our operations (for example, email or hosting providers) or when required by law. We require such parties to protect your data appropriately.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us at info@bestowsecurity.com.
